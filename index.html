What a tracker result proves

No record found

Decisive only when the bank you queried is the institution named on the document as the ordering or beneficiary bank. In that case the payment does not exist in that bank's systems. Against any other bank it means the payment did not pass through that bank, which is not the same thing.

A record found

Confirms a payment carrying that reference exists. It does not confirm that the amount, the beneficiary, the value date or the purpose match the document in front of you. Compare every field.

An acknowledgement

Swift acknowledges that a message was well formed and accepted by the network. It is not a credit, not a settlement, and not proof that money moved. Documents that present an acknowledgement as a completed transfer are the most common form of payment-confirmation fraud.

A screenshot

Proves nothing on its own. Run the query yourself, from a link on this page, and keep the address bar and the timestamp in view. Forged tracker screenshots circulate alongside forged MT103 advices.
